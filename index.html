Hi! A few words about me

I am a results-driven frontend developer with 7 years of commercial experience, delivering accessible, high-performance web applications for international clients across finance, e-commerce, energy, sport and education.

My core expertise lies in the React and TypeScript ecosystem. I turn business requirements into scalable, maintainable products and take ownership end to end — from UI design and architecture, through code reviews and stakeholder demos, to production delivery.

An engineering background — a degree in technical physics (energy conversion) from Gdańsk University of Technology and hands-on work in the renewable-energy sector — gives me an analytical, data-driven approach to solving problems.

I am currently expanding into backend development on my path to becoming a full-stack engineer, with a strong focus on AI-assisted workflows.

01 / 05Aviation

Aviation has been my hobby for years because of the thrill and excitement it brings. The feeling of freedom when flying through the sky is unparalleled. I am fascinated by the technology and engineering behind aircraft. Understanding how planes work and the science of flight captivates me. Visiting airports and watching planes take off and land is a relaxing and inspiring activity. I enjoy reading about aviation history and the evolution of aircraft over time. Flying allows me to see the world from a completely different perspective. It also provides an opportunity to travel and explore new places. The aviation community is welcoming and I have met many interesting people who share my passion. Lastly, the challenge of learning to pilot an aircraft and constantly improving my skills keeps me engaged and motivated.
